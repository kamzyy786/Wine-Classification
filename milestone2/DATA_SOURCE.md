# Data provenance

Aeberhard, S. and Forina, M. (1992). Wine [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5PC7J. Dataset page: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/109/wine. Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

`data/wine.csv` is the unchanged CSV used in Milestone 1: 178 samples, 13 chemical predictors and original cultivar IDs encoded as 0/1/2. It was packaged from scikit-learn load_wine, with no chemical values modified. Physical units are not inferred from undocumented metadata.

The chemical checkpoint is extracted from the prior verified model without changing its chemical weights or preprocessing. `original_checkpoint_sha256` in `models/chemistry.pt` identifies its source checkpoint. `results/metrics.json` records the raw CSV and extracted checkpoint hashes. Original sample assignments are retained in `data/*_wine_ids.csv`.

All customer IDs, purchase events, timestamps, unit prices, quantities, merchant categories, coordinates, declarations, event-to-sample links and transaction anomaly/scenario labels are synthetic. They are generated by `history.py`, using seeds 100/101/102 for train/validation/test. Prices are simulation units, not observed market values. No personal or real payment records are used.

`data/chemical_probabilities.npy` combines out-of-fold probabilities for training samples with the final retained model's validation/test probabilities. Source cultivar labels are used for chemical supervision and generator construction, not as inputs to the transaction models. Transaction anomaly labels are excluded from AE fitting and threshold selection; they are used for evaluation and supervised comparisons.
